# Decisions ledger — agentic-workflows

Append-only. Newest last. One row per **direction change** — a decision that reverses, retires or
re-scopes something already written down. Routine additions do not belong here.

Obligations, both in the **same commit** as the change:

1. **Banner the corpse.** The superseded passage keeps its text and gains, at its head:
   `> ⛔ **SUPERSEDED** by <label> (YYYY-MM-DD). Kept for history — do not build from this.`
   A superseded spec that still reads as live is a trap for the next session.
2. **Close the question.** If a doc asked someone to approve something, the answer is written next
   to it — including `overtaken by events, never approved` when that is the truth.
